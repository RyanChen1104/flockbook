## What's New in v0.5.0

Flockbook now works with Grok Build, shows Claude Code's status line under the conversation, and gets a new Settings window. This release also closes a shell-quoting hole.

### ✨ New

- **Grok Build support.** Flockbook shows Grok Build conversations like Claude Code ones. Its questions and permission prompts appear as cards you answer on the Mac or the phone. The message queue and image paste work too.
- **Grok Build status line.** One switch in **Settings > Agents** turns Grok's status line on or off. Flockbook edits `[ui.status_line]` in Grok's `config.toml` and puts it back exactly when you turn it off. It does not touch a file it cannot read.
- **Claude Code status line.** Your status line shows as tags under the conversation, on the Mac and the phone. Flockbook keeps your own status line command and never loses it when you turn the feature off.
- **Settings has tabs:** General, Agents, Remote and Updates, with compact rows.
- **The toolbar's + button** can open a new tab (as before) or split the focused pane right or down. Choose in **Settings > General**.
- **Terminal colors:** use Flockbook's colors or your Ghostty theme, light and dark. Choose in **Settings > General > Terminal**.
- **Phone app on wide screens** (iPad, landscape): agents on the left, the conversation on the right.

### 🔒 Security

- Shell quoting now works per Unicode character. Before, a `'` followed by a combining mark could end the quotes, so text after it could run as a command. `$` and backticks are quoted too.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

- Workspace monograms: an apostrophe inside a word no longer splits it (`Brandon's Brain` → **BB**), on the Mac and the phone.
- A lone Grok question no longer shows `[1/1]` in its footer.
- On macOS 15, a question card takes your keys again after you go Back from Settings.
- While Settings is open, nothing behind it gets your keys, scroll or answers.
